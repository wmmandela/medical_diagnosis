CLINICAL MOTIVATION

Problem Statement & Clinical Challenges

High Triage Caseloads

  • Primary Care Overload: Triage centers evaluate high patient volumes daily with minimal consultation time.
  • Diagnostic Fatigue: Heavy clinical caseloads increase fatigue during initial symptom intake.

Symptom Overlap

  • Non-Specific Features: Fever, headache, chills, and fatigue are shared across multiple infections.
  • Diagnostic Ambiguity: Malaria, Typhoid, and Dengue share high clinical overlap.

Diagnostic Risks

  • Missed Signs: Secondary features like relative bradycardia or orbital pain can be overlooked.
  • Improper Medication: Treating unconfirmed conditions creates serious patient safety risks.

CORE GOALS

System Objectives & Clinical Impact

Primary System Objectives

  • Structured Symptom Intake: Collect presenting symptoms across 6 major organ systems.
  • Relational Rule Inference: Execute declarative medical rules matching symptoms to disease facts.
  • Sequential Questioning: Query candidate diseases sequentially starting with the least likely candidate.
  • Confirmation Test Integration: Process positive and negative lab outcomes to reach definitive diagnosis.

Clinical Impact & Benefits

  • Reduced Errors: Improves preliminary accuracy by systematically querying secondary clinical signs.
  • High-Yield Lab Selection: Recommends specific high-yield lab investigations (e.g. Widal, Dengue NS1).
  • Treatment Safety Protocol: Displays immediate first-aid, dosage guidelines, and contraindications.
  • Persistent Workstation: Light-themed clinician portal with browser localStorage account persistence.

System Architecture & Core Engine

TRIAGE ALGORITHM

3-Disease Sequential Questioning & Pagination

Sequential Priority Algorithm

  • Top 3 Candidate Lock: Locks top 3 candidate diseases matching initial symptoms.
  • Least-Likely-First Priority: Investigates Disease 3 -> Disease 2 -> Disease 1 to rule out lower candidates early.
  • Binary Action Buttons: Simple Yes and No buttons for rapid data entry during busy triage.

Controlled Pagination UX

  • 5-Item Max Pagination: Displays at most 5 questions per page when total questions exceed 5.
  • Auto Hiding Controls: Hides previous/next buttons automatically when total questions <= 5.
  • Inference Unlock: Unlocks final differential card after completing question rounds.
