## Safety and validation requirements before use with patients

Symptom lists are not diagnostic criteria and are incomplete by design: symptoms
vary by patient, age, pregnancy, comorbidity, setting, and disease severity.
Before deployment in a hospital, a clinical governance group must review every
rule, source, version, test threshold, red-flag pathway, and local guideline;
validate performance prospectively on representative local cases; establish
human oversight and escalation; conduct privacy/security and regulatory review;
and continuously monitor safety, bias, calibration, and rule drift. The user
interface must not present support scores as diagnoses or probabilities.
